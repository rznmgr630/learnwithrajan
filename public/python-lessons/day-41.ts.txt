2. Adding item count to Austin warehouse table.

<b>1. The ACID Transaction Properties:</b>
<ul>
  <li><b>Atomicity:</b> All operations in the transaction complete successfully (`COMMIT`), or all changes are reverted (`ROLLBACK`).</li>

  <li><b>Isolation:</b> Concurrent transactions cannot inspect uncommitted intermediate states.</li>
